# AX object-service memory-pressure diagnosis

The AX outer guard stopped on a retained node `memory.pressure full avg10=0.72` sample, 12 seconds after Temporal business completion. The same sample recorded AX Pod cgroup `full avg10=0`, no cgroup OOM event, no VM OOM kill, and 5,385,986,048 bytes available. The read-only host observer attributed the coincident full-pressure burst to the `objects` Pod container cgroup on the same node. The controller's fail-stop was therefore correct under the unchanged zero-PSI contract; the precise request or background operation in MinIO remains unknown.

The AW host observer recorded 43,636 µs total node-full pressure across 1,380 samples, with no interval reaching 10,000 µs. AX recorded 230,878 µs across 1,398 samples, with 11 intervals at or above 10,000 µs and a maximum 22,517 µs. These totals are diagnostic observations, not a substitute for the node `avg10` guard. The AX `objects` Pod was Burstable, UID `ee7b24cb-bdb7-408a-9ec8-093d2ab5ac00`, with a 128 MiB request and 512 MiB limit. A later cgroup read found `memory.current=536,424,448` of `memory.max=536,870,912`, comprising 283,934,720 bytes anonymous, 168,726,528 file and 82,292,736 kernel memory. `memory.events max=372` and OOM=0 are cumulative since container start; there is no before-AX `max` count, so do not assign those events to AX.

Read-only controls used AX's immutable object prefix and stopped temporary port-forwards after use. One 22,539,322-byte exact-document GET took 0.127 seconds and changed neither `memory.events max` nor PSI-full total. A second bounded traversal stopped after 20 registered objects (4,494,757 bytes, all hashes matched) when PSI-full total rose by 94 µs; `max` and OOM did not change. An idle control showed zero delta over its interval. A further bounded traversal read all 271 registered objects once (148,682,180 bytes, all hashes matched) in 2.911 seconds. Its `memory.current` stayed between 535,875,584 and 536,608,768 bytes, within 1 MiB of the 512 MiB limit; PSI-full total increased 4,453 µs, with no new `memory.events max` or OOM event. These small read-only deltas do not reproduce AX's burst or establish its cause. No object was written or deleted and no workflow was restarted.

The next controlled investigation should capture `objects` cgroup `memory.current`, `memory.stat`, `memory.events` and PSI at subsecond cadence **during a full acceptance window**, with exact Pod/container identity and request timing. Sequential GET replay is insufficient; distinguish concurrent writes, background object-service work and reclaim under the existing limit. Only then choose among resource-limit tuning, cache/workload behavior changes or topology isolation; each changes the operating envelope and needs its own reviewed plan. Do not relax the Q04 node guard, infer adequate capacity from free bytes alone, or promote AX's incomplete terminal evidence. Process and Pod recovery remain unproven for #51.
