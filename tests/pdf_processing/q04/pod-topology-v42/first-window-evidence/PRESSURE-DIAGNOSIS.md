# AQ pressure diagnosis and verification

This diagnosis replays retained AQ samples; it does not re-execute a failed workflow or change an acceptance threshold. Sources are AQ's `vm-controller.jsonl`, host `-node-pressure.jsonl`, Pod `failure-evidence/.../resource-attribution.jsonl`, exact Pod UID/container identity in `pod-pre-inference-gates.json`, and successful AN's retained controller and process samples under `/private/tmp/q04-method-pod-cgroup-20260924-an/`. The comparison to AN is bounded: AN ran a different method-invalidation sequence and at a different time, so it is a counterexample to a simple deterministic overlap rule, not an environment-matched control.

| Hypothesis | Prediction | AQ/AN observation | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AQ Pod experienced the stall | AQ Pod/container cgroup `full total` increases with the node | In the two dominant host samples before stop, AQ Pod rose 14,596 and 21,698 microseconds while node rose 14,643 and 22,677; controller saw Pod and node `full avg10=0.18` | **Supported for stall location**; exact process unknown |
| Pressure came only from another container | AQ Pod cgroup remains flat while a sibling rises | AQ Pod was the dominant matching path; observer container cgroup had zero `full` delta in the event window | Rejected as the sole AQ stop explanation |
| Warm parser/fresh child overlap alone deterministically causes a stop | Comparable overlap always shows full PSI | AQ warm parser was about 1.59 GiB PSS as fresh child grew about 268→540 MiB; AN had about 1.59 GiB parser plus about 503 MiB child without `full avg10` stop | **Not established**; overlap may contribute under different host/reclaim conditions |
| OOM, Pod memory limit, or THP compaction directly stopped AQ | OOM/limit or compaction-stall counters rise at trigger | OOM, `memory.events.max/high` and `compact_stall` stayed zero; Pod memory was about 2.17 GiB of 5 GiB at trigger | Not the observed stop condition; THP allocation effects are not fully excluded |

Within roughly 1.2 seconds around the dominant PSI increments, global `allocstall_movable` rose 60 and `pgscan_direct` rose 8,847 pages; Pod `anon` charge rose 387,842,048 bytes. `compact_stall` rose zero, `thp_fault_alloc` rose four and `thp_fault_fallback` rose two. These VM-wide counters do not identify the allocating task. The Pod-local process samples captured the fresh-child birth and growth, but neither those PSS readings nor the cgroup PSI counter assign the stall to one PID. The node available-memory reading and absence of OOM do not refute PSI: the Linux kernel documents PSI as time tasks actually stall, with `full` measuring simultaneous stalls of all non-idle tasks, and publishes system and cgroup views separately ([kernel PSI documentation](https://docs.kernel.org/accounting/psi.html)). Kernel documentation also identifies high `allocstall` rates as a sign of direct reclaim pressure during allocation bursts ([VM sysctl documentation](https://docs.kernel.org/6.16/admin-guide/sysctl/vm.html)).

The Pod's process record for PID 812 has command SHA-256 `9ca90fdc9543697b8bde9da81653eb0c0403ff51533d4abb8f00c47d276a9cb6`, exactly the SHA-256 of the retained runtime command `/experiment/.venv/bin/python -m pdf_processing.lifecycle_child pdf_processing.ocr` encoded as NUL-separated argv. PID 812 is therefore an OCR child, not the later required-evidence interruption child. It was born about 2.33 seconds before the controller stop, after the second OCR component had completed. The current [OCR implementation](../../../../../src/pdf_processing/ocr.py) checks/renders the page and crop, then constructs `RapidOCR()` for each child. The retained samples do not show which of those operations was executing during the stall. Instrumenting that boundary is narrower than altering parser lifetime or memory limits by assumption.

The retained AN run reached 2,095,304,704 bytes of combined warm-parser/fresh-child PSS in one sample, with Pod memory 2,228,969,472 bytes, yet its maximum controller `full avg10` was zero. AQ's maximum combined sample was 2,131,881,984 bytes with Pod memory 2,369,269,760 bytes. AQ's minimum available node memory was 4,891,844,608 bytes versus AN's 5,143,519,232 bytes. These overlapping ranges do not support a simple memory-capacity or always-on concurrency explanation. They do justify examining allocation bursts and host reclaim as a coupled condition, without changing the current guard.

Per-task delay accounting is disabled (`/proc/sys/kernel/task_delayacct=0`) on the existing kind node, and its kernel tracing event directories are unreadable under the current unprivileged observation path. A per-PID reclaim or allocation-stack claim would require separately reviewed instrumentation. The next implementation choice should preserve the warm-parser and required-relationship acceptance contract, instrument the exact allocation/reclaim seam, and use a new identity for one controlled verification. Repeating AQ unchanged would add little causal evidence and is prohibited by the fail-stop/no-auto-retry rule.

The read-only replay `python3 tests/pdf_processing/q04/pod-topology-v42/first-window-evidence/verify_pressure_diagnosis.py` recomputes the two dominant host samples, reclaim deltas, observer-cgroup exclusion, and AQ/AN overlap comparison from the retained raw files. It passed on 2026-09-25. [Expected measurements](PRESSURE-DIAGNOSIS-VERIFICATION.json).
