# Accept complete canonical candidates in the initial document scope

For the initial document scope, Canonical Acceptance applies to one complete
candidate produced from one Asset's complete Capture Package; components may be
validated and referenced individually but are not independently accepted.
An SOP's main steps and required attachment warnings may jointly determine
usability, so the complete candidate must meet the agreed reuse conditions before
acceptance.

Confirmed in the [Q2 decision record](https://github.com/davidlinnnn/data-ingestion/issues/32#issuecomment-6014492266).
See the [canonical design checkpoint](../design/knowledge-platform-canonical-design.md#q2-complete-candidate-acceptance-boundary)
for the boundary and its open Enrichment/version follow-ups.
