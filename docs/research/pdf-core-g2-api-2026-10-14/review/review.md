## Source and evaluator review

The NCCU crop visibly contains exactly the five central OCR items. The reported bboxes match the breadcrumb, two titles, instruction and link label; no right-side duplicate label can substitute for these central observations. The instruction's corner quotes are a model transcription change. G1 permits whitespace and typographic quote-width normalization; this review does not silently expand that rule to corner-quote style substitution. The original raw response and oracle remain unchanged. Thus content/location recovery is reported separately from the4/5strict count.

The SEAL source crop clearly has reward1when adaptation improves performance and0otherwise, and has a superscript note2after performance in addition to equation label(2). The response expresses those two branches with `cases`, keeps `LM` indexed by theta_t, separates the equation label, and uses `\footnotemark[2]` with a separate marker object anchored to `performance`. It does not turn the note into a mathematical exponent. The crop excludes the note body; the response correctly returns no invented body. Manual source review and simple balanced-delimiter checks support visible-expression fidelity. They do not prove that the intended Markdown renderer accepts `\footnotemark`, nor that the separate note body is linked or delivered. Full G1-E07 acceptance therefore remains outside this crop result.

For the English scan, the two full paragraphs match all16frozen lines after only permitted whitespace/layout-line joining, the printed `re-`/`sources` split, and range-dash normalization. They remain two paragraph blocks in the left column and adjacent in output order. Right-column content occurs later. This spot check does not create a whole-page accuracy oracle or prove the rest of the page error-free.
