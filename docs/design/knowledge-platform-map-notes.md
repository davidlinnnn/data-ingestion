## Confirmed charter — 2026-09-21

The user confirmed both charting rounds in the originating design conversation. These are the map's starting constraints, not completed child-ticket resolutions or architecture-review approval.

- Wiki is the first integration case; Retrieval checks that the canonical boundary is reusable. Use several source documents supporting one Wiki topic, with the same canonical inputs also usable by Retrieval. Cover initial ingestion, source updates, processing-method-only changes, independent projection rebuilds, and source withdrawal. Trace published results to the actual canonical and source versions used.
- Design Source Integration's full handoff, but build the first slice from already captured, versioned files submitted through a controlled interface. Specify identity, metadata, policy provenance, and update/withdrawal delivery. Live connector implementation follows selection of a concrete source.
- Evaluate canonical representation against Paper PDF, SOP Markdown, and PPTX now. This does not expand the PDF core's currently accepted processing support or require Markdown/PPTX workers in its current release.
- Begin with one team/data collection with an explicit authorization boundary. Enforce source authorization ceilings, prevent unauthorized content/existence disclosure, and propagate revocation/withdrawal. Unsupported cross-source ACL semantics must be rejected or explicitly unsupported, never silently widened. This scope is not an exemption from governance, retention, or applicable erasure obligations.
- Ordinary content/method updates are asynchronous: an authorized old Published View may remain available, with observable version/freshness, until its replacement passes validation. Revocation, confirmed deletion, and required withdrawal must not wait for ordinary rebuilding. Their consistency and enforcement mechanisms remain decisions to resolve. Projections need not finish ordinary updates together.
- Each projection owns its materialization workflow and product-specific internals. Design canonical consumption, change notification, lineage, governance, and publication interfaces; do not prescribe Wiki composition or Retrieval chunking.
- Kubernetes is the deployment target and Temporal infrastructure is shared. Request/status storage, processing checkpoint storage, and canonical persistence have distinct responsibilities; this does not require three physical databases. No canonical DB or messaging product is selected by this charter.
