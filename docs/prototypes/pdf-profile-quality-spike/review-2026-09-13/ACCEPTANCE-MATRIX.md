## Exact formula/content handoff checklist

For N2 and N6-F, expected handoff is an explicit conjunction of accepted rules:

1. Identify the exact parsed-result artifact/version and digest plus its local typed reference. N2's diagnostic result SHA is `56844edaaabd8455457d72b273b6eda6dac0e9fa87e97b764cf3b2076825f1de` with`#/texts/7`; N6-F is `b30acfb79c293d16db5fc4a3b727dca8bc18f7b9d8ef84377daffa61a0346553` with`#/texts/15`. These IDs must not be transplanted into another assembled result.
2. Bind immutable source artifact/revision/digest from the native plan, original physical page (book printed page is additional), region in PDF points, origin and rotation0 for these fixtures. Parser uses BOTTOMLEFT; diagnostic crops use TOPLEFT. Persist an explicit transform/recipe rather than guessing from field names.
3. Resolve a readable persisted crop **or** retained source/page bytes with pinned renderer/crop recipe, attribution and integrity. A Pod-local filename, bbox alone, or merely having once made a manual crop is insufficient.
4. Keep empty formula text and interpretation status explicit while retaining occurrence/evidence. Validate meaningful glyphs visually against the source; this does not require LaTeX or reinterpretation.
5. **T06 integration:** retrieve the evidence through the real final-result reference after worker replacement and verify integrity/readability. The diagnostic hashes and images establish a candidate mechanism, not completion of this integration check.

Typed code/content follows the same source/result binding principle. Any future profile-required enrichment must durably finish before complete; unsupported required methods fail explicitly. No generic plugin implementation is implied.

## Decisions for human review (not already approved)

1. **Text representation:** for these exact paragraph examples, what separate treatment of footnote markers, margin labels and typographic variants is acceptable? Whitespace/line-wrap comparison may be declared, but do not silently map minus/Greek/subscripts/superscripts. Source preservation does not automatically excuse incorrect promised text.
2. **Required image text:** should the observed `Skil Proposer` error block this selected figure's content acceptance, or is readable source evidence sufficient for that component's declared role? No percentage tolerance is inferred.
3. **Completeness unit:** approve the corrected paragraph and CodeItem token/source checks as named sample oracles, then review remaining table cells and selected body regions. Neither anchors nor tokens alone claim entire-page completeness.
4. **Scope of immediate qualification:** PPT/multicolumn prose and untested language/domain combinations lack samples; choose a bounded qualified scope without silently claiming them. Prior TC manuals are supplemental, not substitutes for TC paper/book coverage.
